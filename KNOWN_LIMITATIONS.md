# Known limitations

- The provided archives do not include a separate 2019, 2022, or ES-only file; the supplied archive spans are filtered by date and recorded in the run log.
- OHLC bars do not reveal intrabar order, so same-bar barrier hits are ties.
- This is an observational discovery study. It does not establish execution feasibility, economic causation, or out-of-sample validity.
- Missing one-minute bars can make a horizon incomplete; no partial outcomes are substituted.
